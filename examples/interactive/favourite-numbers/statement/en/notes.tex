\textbf{Example 1.} The favourite numbers are $1$, $3$, $4$ and $6$ (the program doesn't know them, it sees only $N = 6$ and the answers to its queries). The program asks about the numbers in order from $1$ to $6$, finds $4$ favourite numbers, prints ``! 4'' and terminates.

\textbf{Example 2.} There are no favourite numbers. The program asks about the numbers out of order --- that is allowed too, --- gets \texttt{NO} to all queries, prints ``! 0'' and terminates.
