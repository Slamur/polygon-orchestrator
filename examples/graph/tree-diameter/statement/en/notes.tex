\textbf{Example 1.}

Consider the structure of the given tree:

\begin{itemize}

\item vertex $2$ is the root;

\item vertices $1$ and $5$ are its children;

\item vertices $3$ and $4$ are the children of vertex $1$.

\end{itemize}

This tree has two diameters, each of which contains $3$ edges:

\begin{itemize}

\item $5 - 2 - 1 - 4$;

\item $5 - 2 - 1 - 3$.

\end{itemize}

The order of printing the vertices $S$ and $E$ does not matter, so any of the answers ``3 5 4'', ``3 4 5'', ``3 5 3'', ``3 3 5'' is considered correct.
