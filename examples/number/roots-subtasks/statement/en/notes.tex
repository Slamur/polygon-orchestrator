\textbf{Example 1.} $2^3 = 8 \le 10 < 27 = 3^3$, so $C = 2$. Also $3^2 = 9 \le 10 < 16 = 4^2$, so $S = 3$.

\textbf{Example 2.} The number $63$ is one less than $64 = 4^3 = 8^2$. So the real cube and square roots of $63$ are just below $4$ and $8$, and their integer parts are $3$ and $7$: $3^3 = 27 \le 63 < 64 = 4^3$ and $7^2 = 49 \le 63 < 64 = 8^2$.
