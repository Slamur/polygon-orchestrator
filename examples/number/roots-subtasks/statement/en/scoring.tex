The problem is scored out of 100 points (the problem consists of subtasks).

Points for a subtask are awarded only if all tests of this subtask
and of the necessary subtasks are passed. The exception is the subtasks marked ``points per test'':
their points are split equally among their tests and awarded for each passed test,
if all tests of the necessary subtasks are passed.
