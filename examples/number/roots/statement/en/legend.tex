You are given a positive integer $N$. Find the integer part (rounded down) of its cube root and the integer part (rounded down) of its square root.

More formally, find:

\begin{itemize}
\item the largest integer $C$ for which $C^3 \le N$ --- the integer part of the cube root of $N$;
\item the largest integer $S$ for which $S^2 \le N$ --- the integer part of the square root of $N$.
\end{itemize}

It is guaranteed that for the given $N$ both numbers $C$ and $S$ exist and are uniquely defined.
